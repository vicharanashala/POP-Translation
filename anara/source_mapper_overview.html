Source Mapper: finding the right document and page for a question

What we set out to do, what we built, what we learned, and what is next. Branch anara, folder anara/. Local testing only; nothing here touches the POP dashboard.

1. The goal

A person types a question, for example "How do I control pink bollworm in cotton?". The system should return the documents most likely to answer it, best first, and name the pages inside each. We are testing this on 533 Punjab agriculture documents before deciding whether to add it to POP.

2. How the search works today

  • "Numbers" means an embedding. A small English model (BAAI bge-small-en-v1.5, runs on an ordinary CPU) turns a piece of text into 384 numbers. Text with similar meaning gets similar numbers, so "foot rot" can match text that never uses those exact words.
  • Chunks stay inside one page, so every result names a real PDF page.
  • A local web page (python anara/app.py, address 127.0.0.1:8050) takes a typed question and lists documents with their pages and a text snippet.

5. Adding keyword search: what we tried

Idea: find the words that make a page special, list which pages hold each word, and let a question's words narrow the area before the meaning search runs, so only a fraction of the chunks is searched. If no question word is in the list, we fall back to searching everything. A page's neighbours (two pages either side) would be included.

AttemptResult
TF-IDF with a cutoff. A word scores high if it is frequent on a page but rare across documents.Failed. Crop words such as wheat and paddy appear in about 40% of documents, so they can never score high. None of the words in our four test questions survived the cutoff.
BM25, a refined version: repeats count less and less, long pages are not favoured, scores never go negative.Adopted. With a rule "word in 2 or more documents, under 60% of them", most test words stayed. But common words still listed 1,000 to 1,600 pages each, so the narrowed area was 9% to 28% of the corpus before adding neighbours.
Top 10 words per page by BM25 alone.Mostly noise: garbled text from PDF extraction (aqi, qon, lei) and words from unrelated documents (student, clerk). Wheat became a keyword on 3 pages, paddy on none. Basmati (72 pages) and bollworm (63) worked well.

6. Where we are, and the open decisions

  • Page counts are very uneven: median 4 pages, mean 38, largest 1,705. 201 documents have 10 pages or fewer but hold only 6% of the pages; 36 documents over 100 pages hold 76%.
  • Current plan: split each long document into equal windows of about 10 pages, so a word concentrated in one stretch of a big book scores higher there than a word spread across the whole book. Short documents stay one window.
  • Limit we found: BM25 scores are capped by how rare a word is, so a fixed cutoff keeps only rare words. Choosing that deliberately means a question like "wheat late sowing" may have no keywords and fall back to the full search.
